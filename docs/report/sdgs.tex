\chapter*{SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS (SDGs) MAPPING}

ARGUS addresses the laundering of proceeds of cyber fraud, a harm that falls hardest on ordinary bank customers and erodes trust in digital payments. The project maps to the following United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.35}
\begin{tabular}{|p{0.30\linewidth}|p{0.60\linewidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{SDG} & \textbf{Contribution of ARGUS} \\ \hline
\textbf{SDG 16} -- Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions (Target 16.4: significantly reduce illicit financial flows) & Detects cross-bank mule rings that launder fraud proceeds, reconstructs the money trail and drafts Suspicious Transaction Reports for compliance officers. \\ \hline
\textbf{SDG 8} -- Decent Work and Economic Growth (Target 8.10: strengthen domestic financial institutions) & Strengthens banks' transaction monitoring and reduces false alarms, protecting trust in digital payments and financial inclusion. \\ \hline
\textbf{SDG 9} -- Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure & Applies graph machine learning, digital-twin simulation and language-model agents to financial-crime infrastructure, with open, reproducible code. \\ \hline
\textbf{SDG 10} -- Reduced Inequalities & Fraud victims are often the least able to absorb losses; faster detection of the laundering chain improves the chance of freezing funds before cash-out. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
